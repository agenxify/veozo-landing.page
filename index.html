How does Veozo work?

Veozo is an all-in-one AI content machine for YouTube and Instagram creators. It discovers trending topics in your niche, generates high-converting AI scripts, creates 1-click eye-catching thumbnails, auto-generates SEO tags, hashtags, and captions, and scores your video virality out of 10 before publishing.

What features does Veozo offer for creators?

Veozo offers 6 core AI tools: 1) Trend Discovery (find trending niche topics), 2) AI Script Writer (generate engaging scripts), 3) SEO Optimizer (auto generate viral tags and captions), 4) AI Thumbnail Generator (1-click YouTube thumbnails), 5) AI Keywords & Tags (high reachability hashtags), and 6) AI Video Score (score video out of 10 on virality).

How does the AI Video Score work?

Our AI Video Score evaluates your video's hook strength, pacing, thumbnail click-through rate, and SEO keyword placement to output a virality rating out of 10 before you post.

Which platforms are supported?

Veozo is optimized for YouTube (Long-form and Shorts), Instagram (Reels and Posts), TikTok, and major social video platforms.
