Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our plans? We've got answers.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan anytime? +
Yes! You can adjust or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your dashboard settings. If you upgrade, your new AI limits apply immediately with prorated billing.
What happens if I reach my monthly AI thumbnail limit? +
If you hit your monthly AI thumbnail generation limit before your reset date, you can either upgrade your plan or purchase an add-on thumbnail pack directly from your billing dashboard.
How does the 7-day free trial for Starter work? +
When you choose Starter, you get full access to all Starter features for 7 days with zero upfront charge. You can cancel anytime before the 7 days end without being charged.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or educational creators? +
We offer a 30% discount for registered students, educators, and non-profit organizations. Reach out to support@veozo.app with your credentials to get your discount code.
