Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about contacting support and enterprise options.

How quickly does Veozo support respond? +
Our customer support operates 24/7. Pro and Enterprise customers receive priority response within 15 minutes. Free tier users typically receive a reply within 2 hours.
Can I request a custom 1-on-1 demo for my agency? +
Yes! Select "Enterprise Sales" in the contact form or email enterprise@veozo.app. Our solutions engineers will schedule a live demonstration tailored to your team's workflow.
Do you offer API access for bulk video processing? +
Yes, Veozo provides high-throughput REST APIs for automated video clipping, transcription, and metadata generation. Contact enterprise sales for API documentation and custom quota tiers.
Where can I submit feature requests or report bugs? +
You can submit bugs directly through the contact form above, or join our official Creator Discord community to vote on upcoming features and talk directly with our product team.
